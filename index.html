Why the two lists disagree

  • Why each Global tier (the audit's reasons, from two measures: NotMeter's score and damage at equal combat power). Chanter S: top on both, plus the best party buffs. Ranger A: a high score, and top Rangers also have the highest median combat power. Gladiator A: strong on both, and it can also tank. Sorcerer A: 2nd at equal combat power. Assassin B: mid, with a big skill ceiling (the top 10% play about 83% above the combat-power trend). Templar B as DPS, S as the only real tank: Taunt's Shrink and Fury raise the whole party's damage. Spiritmaster B−: Global's low cooldown reduction caps the Ancient Spirit. Cleric C as DPS, S as the healer: NotMeter measures damage only.
  • Chanter is #1 on Global and 8th in Korea. Global players have skills at 12–16, and the Chanter's no-cooldown Dark Crush (skill level 16) is a huge jump that doesn't need much else. Korea's endgame adds 6 stigmas at level 25, ~35–40% cooldown reduction and 2–5 skills at 20, which the crit and cooldown classes (Assassin, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Ranger) scale with far better. Chanter also spends part of its kit on party buffs, which don't show in its own number.
  • Assassin is mid on Global and #1 (of Global's 8) in Korea. Its damage stacks multiplicatively on crit, crit damage and back-attack stats that are still low on Global.
  • Templar and Cleric aren't DPS picks. Templar is the only real tank on Global (in 64% of boss parties), and Cleric is the healer (in 89% of fights). Gladiators tank the Templar-less parties without changing their build, and Chanters cover the Cleric-less ones. "DPS Templar" and "DPS Cleric" aren't separate specs on Global. Their numbers are here for completeness.
  • Two Global rules reshape stats. The crit cap is 50%, and top Gladiators, Rangers and Assassins already sit at it, so more Crit is wasted for them. Cooldown reduction tops out around 19–21%, which hurts Sorcerer and Spiritmaster most (Spiritmaster drops to 7th).
  • PvP and solo tiers are UNVERIFIED. There is no Global PvP ranking: launch-week opinion pieces put Assassin and Gladiator on top, and Korean PvP rankings don't transfer to Global's 4 stigma slots. For solo and leveling (opinion, no Global speed data): A Ranger, Gladiator, Chanter, Spiritmaster; B+ Sorcerer; B Assassin, Templar, Cleric.
  • Spread inside each class is large: the top 10% of every class deal 35–70% more than their combat power predicts, so how you play moves you more than which class you pick. See each class's Max damage plan.

Pick: Chanter, then Gladiator

  • Chanter is the top Global DPS at equal gear right now (+19% vs the average class this week, +22% in the audit's Oct 7–14 week), and about 97% of its damage runs from one macro key plus held left click: no charge skills, no ground targeting, and Dark Crush hits from 20 m. Parties want its buffs too. What you have to do: push Dark Crush to skill level 16 first (option 5), then Onslaught to 16, and keep Dark Crush casts high. Catch: in Korea's endgame it drops to the bottom of the DPS list, so it's the best pick for Season 1, not forever.
  • Gladiator is A tier with nearly 100% of its damage automatable: one skill (Overhead Slam + Upward Strike) is 61% of it, and the macro alternates it with Rending Blow for you. You stand in front (77% front hits) and in melee.
  • Templar is almost as simple (Judgment is 42.5% on one macro row) but it's the tank, and Punishment (11.5%) has to be charged by hand.
  • Spiritmaster is the audit's runner-up for ease: Sirin calls it a low-skill-floor class that "performs very well on high ping", and about a third of its damage lands whatever you press. Its Global damage is lower (B−).
  • Hardest to max: Sorcerer (Hellfire charge, two ground AoEs and six skills at 6% or more each), Ranger (Deadshot charge, 23.5%), Assassin (85% of hits must land from behind). All three are top-tier in Korea's endgame, so the effort pays off later.

How a slot stack works

key

Each hotbar key has four rows above it, numbered 0–3. Pressing the key casts the lowest-numbered skill that's ready. Row 0 goes first, and row 3 is the filler. (Row 0 first is how the Korean setups work; no source states the firing order outright and it hasn't been tested on Global, so treat it as UNVERIFIED.)

A macro entry points to a slot, not a skill. When the macro reaches that entry, it uses the whole stack in priority order. The hotbar icon shows whichever skill is ready next.

Set it up (TW client, Global names in brackets)

  1. Press K. Drag skills into the rows above a hotbar key in the order on the class page (row 0 at the bottom).
  2. Click 基本技能輔助功能 [Macro], then 新增輔助功能 [Add Macro]. Click the entry's icon, pick the slot you stacked, and set 延遲 [Delay] to 10 ms.
  3. Bind the key: 設定 → 按鍵設定 → 一般 [Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro]. Most players use a mouse button.
  4. Play in Target mode. Hold basic attack + macro key (plus any third key the class page lists). Check your numbers on the training dummy with the DPS meter (Ctrl+X).
  5. Set 技能預約 [Skill queue] as the class page says. If skills stutter or get skipped, flip it and test again.

Rules the top players agree on

  • Fewer different lines is better. Every extra entry cuts the turns each slot gets. Repeating your main line (Sorcerer's in-game setup lists it 3 times out of 6) keeps its share up.
  • Never stack two skills with the same cooldown in one slot. They block each other.
  • Toggles never go in a macro (Chanter mantras): a second press switches them off.
  • Keep charge skills manual unless the class page holds that slot on purpose.
  • Only raise the delay if skills get skipped. 10 ms is standard.
  • Mouse-software macros (Logitech, Razer hold-to-run) top the Korean meters, but on Global they are a ban risk: NC's policy bans programs or devices that enable actions impossible for regular players. Its Oct 10 notice (4,996 accounts) named "use of macros, bots or other unauthorized programs", and the Oct 12 (10,000) and Oct 14 (9,422) waves gave the same reasons. Allyria's FAQ calls hold-to-run "not bannable", but that isn't an NC statement. Korea patched against mouse macros on Sep 16 (live on Global). Every class page has an in-game-only setup, the in-game macro is allowed, and no Global patch through Oct 13 has nerfed any class macro.
  • Check ping and FPS before blaming the macro. In Kanon's tests, a 150–200 ms ping gap was worth roughly 50–70% damage.

When a second macro line helps, and when it hurtsReportedly up to 20 entries of up to 4 skills each

Why extra entries cost damage

Holding the key walks the list top to bottom, weaves a basic attack, then loops. Each entry is one turn in that loop. With one entry, your row-0 skill is checked every turn; with two different entries, every other turn. Korean testers all saw the same thing: each added line lowers the hits every slot gets.

Gladiator, Overhead Slam hits per minute. Adding Sword Aura Rampage as a second entry cost 5–6% (Inven, Aug 12).

Good reasons for more entries

  1. Skills keep colliding. Chanter: moving Impactful Crush to its own entry gave +3–5 Dark Crush casts/min (Sep 3).
  2. A group runs on its own timer. Spiritmaster's four spirits get their own line.
  3. A cancel needs a skill on a different line. Ranger's Tempest Shot cancel (~+3%). Gladiator's one-key setup alternates a Rending Blow line and an Overhead Slam line so the 내절 cancel happens on its own.
  4. It automates damage you'd otherwise miss. Sorcerer's ground AoEs are about a quarter of its damage, so the in-game setup puts them in the macro.
  5. Cleric: its attack-speed cap means two lines cost almost nothing.

Rows inside a stack

Row 0: the skill that hurts most when delayed: a short proc window (Judgment, Heart Gore, Sword Aura Rampage) or a long-cooldown hit everything else benefits from. Rows 1–2: the other cooldown skills, by value. Row 3: a no-cooldown filler that plugs every gap.

Usually keep out of the macro

  • Buffs that should wait for the boss. Automated buffs fire on trash and during mechanics.
  • Skills you mash faster than the loop can (Spiritmaster's Elemental Fusion).
  • Charge skills (Punishment, Bolt, Deadshot, Hellfire): hold or press them on their own key.

Damage basicsWhat actually moves your numbers · summarized from Kanon's Aion 2 Bible (Sep 20, 2026)

Where skill levels come from

  • Skill points (Wisdom Stones) take an active skill to 10. Everything above that is bonus levels.
  • Active skills: ring lines, blue Daevanion tiles, and Parchment / Compass / Chalice Arcana cards. The Bible also lists weapon and guard lines, but no Global weapon or guard line list includes them, so that is UNVERIFIED on Global.
  • Passives: armor, necklace and earring lines, green Daevanion tiles, and Bell / Mirror / Chalice Arcana cards.
  • Level 8: first 3 specialty options and 1 slot. 12: option 4 and a 2nd slot. 16: option 5. 20: the 3rd slot.
  • Korean endgame has 2–5 skills at 20 depending on class. Gladiator's Overhead Slam (with its Upward Strike chain) is 61% of its damage and Templar's Judgment is 42.5%, so those skills go first. Season 1 has only 5 Arcana cards and 4–5 gear lines, so few skills reach 20 on Global.

Which stats are worth the most

  • Double Chance > Front/Back Damage Boost > Weapon Damage Boost ≈ Crit Damage Boost > Damage Boost.
  • Flat attack: Attack > Max Attack > Attack Bonus > PvE / Boss Attack.
  • Korean endgame values per 1%: Double Chance ≈ 0.6%, Front/Back ≈ 0.6%, Weapon Damage Boost ≈ 0.5%, Crit Damage ≈ 0.4–0.6%, plain Damage Boost ≈ 0.35%. At launch, with fewer stats, each point is worth more.
  • Classes with a crit-damage passive (Gladiator, Chanter, Ranger, Assassin, Cleric) gain a bit less from extra Crit Damage Boost.
  • Bosses have no Front/Back, Weapon, Crit or Multi-hit stats, so those tolerances are worthless in PvE.
  • Multi-hit: each extra hit is 5% of the hit, so 1% multi-hit ≈ 0.33% damage.

What this means for your macro

  • Ping and FPS matter a lot. Macros are hit-count setups: a 150–200 ms ping difference was worth roughly 50–70% damage. If your hits per minute are far under the class page's targets, check ping first.
  • Combat Speed is the stat to stack; more hits also means more procs from everything else.
  • Crit caps at 50% on Global (80% in Korea; the training dummy caps around 8%). Once a skill crits at the cap, a "+crit" specialty does nothing, so take a different pick. This is also why Precision lines matter less on Global. Top Global Gladiators, Rangers and Assassins already sit at the cap, so Crit stats are wasted for them.
  • Accuracy works the same way against a boss's Block. Projected Global Ludra: ~1,200 Accuracy and 1,200–1,400 Crit. Korean raids: Corroded 2,350 / 2,500; Muspel Hard 2,800 / 3,150.
  • Potential stats are PvP-only on Global, and PvE Damage Boost was removed from weapons, guards and accessories; ignore Potential for PvE.
  • Power Shards: regular ≈ +4.7% damage, Superior ≈ +8.9%.

Source: Kanon's Aion 2 Bible (twitch.tv/kanonxo), Oct 4 Global update. The per-1% values are Korean endgame numbers, and the author marks several as unconfirmed for Global.

Manastones, Soulstones, Pantheon

  • Manastones (weapons and armor) and Soulstones (accessories) roll random lines and are lost when you transfer gear, so don't sink Superiors into gear you'll replace.
  • Stone lines, best to worst: Front/Back Damage Boost > Weapon Damage Boost ≈ Crit Damage Boost > Damage Boost > Attack. Aim for at least one yellow Damage Boost line (yellow = 100 = 1%).
  • Each Pantheon stat gives two effects. Damage picks: Destruction (Attack Increase), Wisdom (Double Chance), Illusion (cooldowns), Time (Combat Speed), Death (Crit). Defensive: Justice, Space, Freedom, Life, Destiny.
  • Bracelet: Illusion, Wisdom, Destruction, Time, plus Justice and Space for PvE (Korea's Sep guides).
  • Theostones add very little damage; pick the one you like the look of.

Arcana, Daevanion boards, Pet Genus

  • Arcana at Global launch: 5 cards, 2 sets. Kanon's pick: Primal Vigor on Chalice (Time) + Parchment (Life) for +60 PvE Attack above 70% HP, and Magic Armor on Compass (Death), Bell (Destruction) + Mirror (Wisdom) for an MP refill. The three lines that matter most: Chalice Time (Combat Speed), Bell Destruction (Attack Increase), Mirror Wisdom (Double Chance).
  • Later (8 cards, what Korea's top 20 run in every class): Punishing Overture (4: +60 PvE Attack, +10% PvE damage above 70% HP) + Protected Soul (4: +5% Weapon Damage Boost).
  • Get your two key actives to 20 first, even with a cheaper card set, then craft 4-line cards (Chalice first: it rolls from every skill, so it's the hardest to get perfect). Each class page lists which skills Korea's top players put on Arcana and rings.
  • Daevanion: the four white boards (Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel, Triniel) share points and give skill levels: take your key active tiles first (to reach 12 / 16 / 20), then orange tiles, and pick up every gray Attack Bonus tile eventually. Wings, enhancing, transfers

  • Wings give an equip effect and an owned effect. They can't be enhanced in Season 1, and Talisra wings (Season Shop) are Global's cooldown-reduction goal. Whether Korea's Eroded and Awakened Pride wings drop on Global is UNVERIFIED (Sirin: wings now drop only from dungeon cubes). Korea's most-equipped wings (Aug): Eroded for front attackers (Gladiator 73%, Templar 77%, Ranger 55%, Cleric and Chanter 45%), Talisra for Sorcerer (92%) and many Spiritmasters, Talisra → Awaken Pride (back attack) for Assassin.
  • Enhancing: yellow (Unique) gear goes to +15 then 5 Amp levels; orange (Heroic) goes to +20 then 5 Amp levels, but Global Season 1 has no Heroic gear (Amp levels add orange stats). A yellow +15 Amp 5 transfers into an orange piece at +20 Amp 0.
  • Settle rarity: Unique (yellow) for every slot in Season 1, except bracelet slot 2, which is a blue (Epic) bracelet. Crafted gear (5 lines) is best in slot and dungeon gear has 4 (an extendable weapon has 3 lines per Sirin or 4 per Kanon's Bible: UNVERIFIED which applies); F2P or sub-only players can stay in dungeon armor "without losing a lot" (Allyria). Take the belt and amulet to Gold early, then stop for now (a Heroic belt or amulet in Season 1 is UNVERIFIED). Pendant, Brooch and Seals aren't on Global yet.
  • Clash Runes (class runes) are the only gear that can break when enhancing: a failed upgrade destroys the rune. The sheet says +1 early on and about +5 before Ludra, Allyria says not past +6/7 because upgrades are RNG-heavy, and top Korean players sit around +7.
  • Transfers: Manastones, Soulstones, Theostones, Potential lines and the extra Philosopher's Stone line are lost. Orange transfer fragments are scarce (1 orange piece = 5 fragments; a weapon transfer needs 20), so hoard them. Kanon skips the first orange set (Ancient Spirit) and goes orange on the second (Faded Shadow); orange (Heroic) gear is not in Global Season 1, so that is for later.
