Levels 16–20 cost as much as 1–15 combined. That's why every priority list gets several stigmas to their cheap unlocks (5 or 10) before pushing anything to 20.

Advanced shards (levels 21–25)

  • +1 for each character level from 46 to 50 (5 total).
  • +1 for every stigma you take to 20 (refunded if you reset it).
  • More are sold in the Abyss exchange shop.
  • With 6 stigmas at 20 you have 11: two at 25 + one at 21.
  • The ceiling is all 13 at 20 (975 shards), which gives 18: three at 25 + one at 23. That's what "Lv 23" means in the builds.

Slots and rules

  • Stigmas unlock at character level 22. Each class has 13.
  • 6 slots in KR/TW since the Jul 1 Chapter 1 update (4 at launch, 5 from March). Global may start with fewer: take picks in the numbered order.
  • Same-name party buffs don't stack: two Chanters' mantras, Undefeated Mantra vs Light of Protection, Power of the Storm vs Earth's Blessing, two Sorcerers' Cold Storm. The higher one wins.
  • Sep 23: during Abyss boss fights, Rifts and Time-Space battles, Battlefield Might replaces party synergies (Undefeated Mantra, Light of Protection, Power of the Storm and others do nothing there).
