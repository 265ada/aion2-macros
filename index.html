Roadmap

  1. Level to 45 on the main story. Along the way (or after): Sealed Dungeons (Daevanion crystals + skill points), Regional Quests (crystals + gear), Feathers (188 total: amulet scrolls, stamina, amplification shards), Strongholds (belt scrolls), and Rifting into the enemy faction (PvP can be toggled).
  2. At 45: go to the Abyss for feathers and weekly quests, buy a ring with Abyss Points, and finish leftover side content.
  3. Vakron Sky Island (2★): every piece +10 with Manastones (~1,400 score). Farm until you have the full set. At 7/8 and far from pity, run the expedition version.
  4. Deus Stage 1–2: all green, then all blue Arcana cards, ideally with your main skills to +16. Level belt and amulet (~1,920 → ~2,190 score).
  5. Kromede for accessories. Craft the Splendent Dark Dragon Lord weapon, or use the Kromede weapon until you can.
  6. Ferocious Horn Den for guard and armor (the armor lasts all of Season 1), all +10 (~2,560).
  7. Deus Stage 3–4: all yellow cards with the right skills to reach level 20 for Ludra.

Ludra prep: weapon, guard and rings +17; earring and necklace +16; armor and bracelet +15; class runes about +5. Systems to work on: stigma and skill points, Daevanion board, soul binding, Pet Genus, Pantheon, crafted weapon / guard / accessories. Boss guide videos: Vakron · Deus Stage 1 · Kromede · Ferocious Horn Den.

Global is not Korea (yet)

  • Stigmas: 4 slots that open at character level 22, 27, 32 and 37, with a max level of 20. A fresh level 45 has about 32 stigma shards (Sirin's Global guide).
  • Cooldown reduction is far lower: the best Global gear measured ~19–21% on the launch-scale test, against 34–40% in Korea. Korean macros that rely on cooldown alignment (Sorcerer, Spiritmaster) run looser.
  • Divine (Pantheon) stats count half: 10 points = 1% instead of 5. Titles lost Smite / Double Chance, and Combat Speed and cooldown titles are ~30% lower.
  • Gear stat values are roughly halved, and Pet Genus changed the most, so use the Global Genus lines on each class page.
  • Arcana starts with 5 cards (Chalice, Parchment, Compass, Bell, Mirror).
  • Smite = Double Chance: Global guides use both names for the same stat (a 2× damage proc).

How a slot stack works

Each hotbar key has four rows above it, numbered 0–3. Pressing the key casts the lowest-numbered skill that's ready. Row 0 goes first, and row 3 is the filler.

A macro entry points to a slot, not a skill. When the macro reaches that entry, it uses the whole stack in priority order. The hotbar icon shows whichever skill is ready next.

Set it up (TW client, Global names in brackets)

  1. Press K. Drag skills into the rows above a hotbar key in the order on the class page (row 0 at the bottom).
  2. Click 基本技能輔助功能 [Macro], then 新增輔助功能 [Add Macro]. Click the entry's icon, pick the slot you stacked, and set 延遲 [Delay] to 10 ms.
  3. Bind the key: 設定 → 按鍵設定 → 一般 [Settings → Key Settings → General → Skill Macro]. Most players use a mouse button.
  4. Play in Target mode. Hold basic attack + macro key (plus any third key the class page lists). Check your numbers on the training dummy with the DPS meter (Ctrl+X).
  5. Set 技能預約 [Skill queue] as the class page says. If skills stutter or get skipped, flip it and test again.

Rules the top players agree on

  • Fewer different lines is better. Every extra entry cuts the turns each slot gets. Repeating your main line (Sorcerer's in-game setup lists it 3 times out of 6) keeps its share up.
  • Never stack two skills with the same cooldown in one slot. They block each other.
  • Toggles never go in a macro (Chanter mantras): a second press switches them off.
  • Keep charge skills manual unless the class page holds that slot on purpose.
  • Only raise the delay if skills get skipped. 10 ms is standard.
  • Mouse-software macros (Logitech, Razer) top the Korean meters. NC patched against them on Sep 16, and every class page has an in-game-only setup.
  • Check ping and FPS before blaming the macro. In Kanon's tests, a 150–200 ms ping gap was worth roughly 50–70% damage.

When a second macro line helps, and when it hurtsReportedly up to 20 entries of up to 4 skills each

Why extra entries cost damage

Holding the key walks the list top to bottom, weaves a basic attack, then loops. Each entry is one turn in that loop. With one entry, your row-0 skill is checked every turn; with two different entries, every other turn. Korean testers all saw the same thing: each added line lowers the hits every slot gets.

Gladiator, Overhead Slam hits per minute. Adding Sword Aura Rampage as a second entry cost 5–6% (Inven, Aug 12).

Good reasons for more entries

  1. Skills keep colliding. Chanter: moving Impactful Crush to its own entry gave +3–5 Dark Crush casts/min (Sep 3).
  2. A group runs on its own timer. Spiritmaster's four spirits get their own line.
  3. A cancel needs a skill on a different line. Ranger's Tempest Shot cancel (~+3%). Gladiator's one-key setup alternates a Rending Blow line and an Overhead Slam line so the 내절 cancel happens on its own.
  4. It automates damage you'd otherwise miss. Sorcerer's ground AoEs are about a quarter of its damage, so the in-game setup puts them in the macro.
  5. Cleric: its attack-speed cap means two lines cost almost nothing.

Rows inside a stack

Row 0: the skill that hurts most when delayed: a short proc window (Judgment, Heart Gore, Sword Aura Rampage) or a long-cooldown hit everything else benefits from. Rows 1–2: the other cooldown skills, by value. Row 3: a no-cooldown filler that plugs every gap.

Usually keep out of the macro

  • Buffs that should wait for the boss. Automated buffs fire on trash and during mechanics.
  • Skills you mash faster than the loop can (Spiritmaster's Elemental Fusion).
  • Charge skills (Punishment, Bolt, Deadshot, Hellfire): hold or press them on their own key.

Global Season 1

  • 4 stigma slots (confirmed on NC's official livestream) and a stigma level cap of 20, like Korea's launch season (Nov 2025 – Mar 2026).
  • No level 21–25 and no Advanced shards yet. Korea added the 5th slot on Mar 25 and the 6th slot plus level 25 on Jul 1, so expect Global to follow later.
  • Effects are read from the Global client database. It has Korea's Aug 26 rework but not the Sep 16 patch.
  • Stigmas unlock at character level 22. Each class has 13. Every effect at 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 is automatic; there are no picks.
  • Same-name party buffs don't stack: two Chanters' mantras, Undefeated Mantra vs Light of Protection, Power of the Storm vs Earth's Blessing, two Sorcerers' Cold Storm. The higher one wins.

KR/TW: Advanced shards (21–25)

  • +1 for each character level from 46 to 50 (5 total).
  • +1 for every stigma you take to 20 (refunded if you reset it).
  • More are sold in the Abyss exchange shop.
  • With 6 stigmas at 20 you have 11: two at 25 + one at 21. The ceiling (all 13 at 20, 975 shards) gives three at 25 + one at 23.
  • Sep 23 (KR/TW): during Abyss boss fights, Rifts and Time-Space battles, Battlefield Might replaces party synergies (Undefeated Mantra, Light of Protection, Power of the Storm, Fury, Experienced Counterstrike and others do nothing there).

Damage basicsWhat actually moves your numbers · summarized from Kanon's Aion 2 Bible (Sep 20, 2026)

Where skill levels come from

  • Skill points (Wisdom Stones) take an active skill to 10. Everything above that is bonus levels.
  • Active skills: ring lines, weapon and guard lines, blue Daevanion tiles, and Parchment / Compass / Chalice Arcana cards.
  • Passives: armor, necklace and earring lines, green Daevanion tiles, and Bell / Mirror / Chalice Arcana cards.
  • Level 8: first 3 specialty options and 1 slot. 12: option 4 and a 2nd slot. 16: option 5. 20: the 3rd slot.
  • Korean endgame has 2–5 skills at 20 depending on class. Gladiator (Overhead Slam) and Templar (Judgment) get over 60% of their damage from one skill, so that skill goes first.

Which stats are worth the most

  • Double Chance > Front/Back Damage Boost > Weapon Damage Boost ≈ Crit Damage Boost > Damage Boost.
  • Flat attack: Attack > Max Attack > Attack Bonus > PvE / Boss Attack.
  • Korean endgame values per 1%: Double Chance ≈ 0.6%, Front/Back ≈ 0.6%, Weapon Damage Boost ≈ 0.5%, Crit Damage ≈ 0.4–0.6%, plain Damage Boost ≈ 0.35%. At launch, with fewer stats, each point is worth more.
  • Classes with a crit-damage passive (Gladiator, Chanter, Ranger, Assassin, Cleric) gain a bit less from extra Crit Damage Boost.
  • Bosses have no Front/Back, Weapon, Crit or Multi-hit stats, so those tolerances are worthless in PvE.
  • Multi-hit: each extra hit is 5% of the hit, so 1% multi-hit ≈ 0.33% damage.

What this means for your macro

  • Ping and FPS matter a lot. Macros are hit-count setups: a 150–200 ms ping difference was worth roughly 50–70% damage. If your hits per minute are far under the class page's targets, check ping first.
  • Combat Speed is the stat to stack; more hits also means more procs from everything else.
  • Crit caps at 80%. If a skill already crits at the cap, a "+crit" specialty does nothing, so take a different pick.
  • Accuracy works the same way against a boss's Block. Korean raid targets (not confirmed for Global): Ludra 1,500 Accuracy / 1,600 Crit; Corroded 2,350 / 2,500; Muspel Hard 2,800 / 3,150.
  • Power Shards: regular ≈ +4.7% damage, Superior ≈ +8.9%.

Source: Kanon's Aion 2 Bible (twitch.tv/kanonxo). The numbers are Korean endgame values, and the author marks several as unconfirmed for Global.

Manastones, Soulstones, Pantheon

  • Manastones (weapons and armor) and Soulstones (accessories) roll random lines and are lost when you transfer gear, so don't sink Superiors into gear you'll replace.
  • Stone lines, best to worst: Front/Back Damage Boost > Weapon Damage Boost ≈ Crit Damage Boost > Damage Boost > Attack. Aim for at least one yellow Damage Boost line (yellow = 100 = 1%).
  • Each Pantheon stat gives two effects. Damage picks: Destruction (Attack Increase), Wisdom (Double Chance), Illusion (cooldowns), Time (Combat Speed), Death (Crit). Defensive: Justice, Space, Freedom, Life, Destiny.
  • Bracelet: Illusion, Wisdom, Destruction, Time, plus Justice and Space for PvE (Korea's Sep guides).
  • Theostones add very little damage; pick the one you like the look of.

Arcana, Daevanion boards, Pet Genus

  • Arcana cards give Pantheon stats and skill levels. Kanon expects Global to start like Asia: 5 cards (Chalice, Parchment, Compass, Bell, Mirror) and 2 sets. Key lines: Chalice Time (Combat Speed), Bell Destruction, Mirror Wisdom.
  • Get your two key actives to 20 first, even with a cheaper card set, then craft 4-line cards (Chalice first).
  • Daevanion: the four white boards (Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel, Triniel) share points and give skill levels. Take your key active tiles first, then orange tiles. Multi-hit Resist (Triniel) and Crit Damage Tolerance (Vaizel) are useless in PvE. Respec is nearly free.
  • Pet Genus: level each board to 10 before locking any line, then roll for Double Chance and Front/Back Damage Boost lines.
