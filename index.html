Chaining: when a second 4-stack helps and when it hurtsReportedly up to 20 entries of up to 4 skills each. More entries is usually slower.

Why extra entries cost damage

Holding the key walks the list top to bottom, weaves a basic attack, then loops. Each entry is one turn in that loop. With one entry, your row-0 skill is checked every turn. With two entries it's checked every other turn, so a proc skill like Heart Gore, Judgment or Sword Aura Rampage waits longer to fire and the cancel windows between entries get longer. Korean testers all saw the same thing: each added line lowers the hits every slot gets.

Gladiator, Overhead Slam hits per minute. Adding Sword Aura Rampage as a second entry cost 5–6% (Inven, Aug 12).

Add a second entry only for one of these reasons

  1. Two skills in the stack keep colliding. Chanter: Spinning Strike, Impactful Crush and Marchutan's Wrath all open Dark Crush windows. Stacked together, their windows overlap and get wasted. Moving Impactful Crush to its own entry gave +3–5 Dark Crush casts/min and about +100–150k DPS in Expedition (Inven, Sep 3).
  2. A group runs on its own timer. Spiritmaster spirits (15 s each) get their own line. Putting that line in the macro tested better than mashing it by hand (Sep 16).
  3. A cancel needs a skill on a different line. Ranger's Tempest Shot has to sit in a different line from the short-cooldown arrows to get the Tempest Shot cancel (~+3%). The Ranger ceiling is exactly two entries: the Drill Dart line and the damage line (Sep 3).
  4. Cleric only: its low attack-speed cap means two lines cost almost nothing (Sep 24).

Never chain these

  • A buff line. Ranger Snipe drops below 16–18/min, and Gladiator loses hits across the board. Press buffs yourself.
  • Skills you mash faster than the loop can. Gladiator Overhead Slam and Spiritmaster Elemental Fusion. Mash them on their own key.
  • Assassin Ambush in any line. Hits dropped in testing.
  • Charge skills (Punishment, Bolt, Deadshot, Rushing Smash). Hold or press them on their own key.

Rows inside a stack

Row 0: the skill that hurts most when delayed, meaning a short proc window (Judgment, Heart Gore, Sword Aura Rampage) or a long-cooldown hit that everything else benefits from. Rows 1–2: the other cooldown skills, by value. Row 3: a filler with no cooldown that plugs every gap. Never put two skills with the same cooldown in one stack.

Each hotbar key has four rows above it, numbered 0–3. Pressing the key casts the lowest-numbered skill that's ready. Row 0 goes first, and row 3 is the filler.

A macro entry points to a slot, not a skill. The name in the macro window is just the slot's representative. When the macro reaches that entry, it uses every skill in that stack in priority order.

The hotbar icon changes to whichever skill in the stack is ready next. That's why your key can show a different skill than the macro list.

Set it up on the TW clientGlobal client names are in brackets

  1. Press K. In the skill window, drag skills into the rows above a hotbar key, in the order on the card (row 0 at the bottom).
  2. Click 基本技能輔助功能 [Macro] at the top right, then 新增輔助功能 [Add Macro]. A basic-attack entry appears. Click its icon and pick the slot you stacked. Set 延遲 [Delay] to 10 ms.
  3. Bind the key: 設定 → 按鍵設定 → 一般 [Settings → Key Settings → General]. Most players use a mouse side button.
  4. Play in Target mode and pick a target. Hold basic attack + macro key, plus the third key if the class card lists one. Check your numbers on the training dummy with the in-game DPS meter (Ctrl+X).
  5. Set 技能預約 [skill queue] as the class card says. If skills stutter or get skipped, flip it and test again.

Rules the top players agree onFrom Korean and Taiwanese forum testing

  • Use one macro line if you can. Every extra entry cuts the hits each slot gets. Sorcerer loses the most, and Cleric can afford two.
  • Never stack two skills with the same cooldown in one slot. They block each other and add delay.
  • Split your two biggest hitters into different slots if they share cast time (Cleric: Condemnation vs Judgment Thunder).
  • Keep charge skills manual unless the guide holds that slot on purpose (Sorcerer's Hellfire line is built to be held).
  • Only raise the delay if skills get skipped. 10 ms is standard, and the TW Cleric setup uses 40/20/20.
  • Mouse-software macros (Razer, G Hub) top the Korean DPS meters. NC patched against them on Sept 16, so this sheet treats them as optional.
  • Check ping and FPS before blaming the macro. A macro is a hit-count setup, and in Kanon's tests a 150–200 ms ping gap was worth roughly 50–70% damage. Combat Speed is the stat that adds hits.
  • Level the skills the macro leans on. Each class's first two skills to 20, target skill levels and stat priority are on the Stigmas & Skills page.
